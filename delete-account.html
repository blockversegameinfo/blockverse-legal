BLOCKVERSE Account Deletion

Users can initiate account deletion from the BLOCKVERSE app Settings.

Unable to access the app?

If you no longer have access to BLOCKVERSE, request account deletion by emailing:

support.blockverse@gmail.com

Subject: BLOCKVERSE ACCOUNT DELETION REQUEST

Please include the email address associated with your BLOCKVERSE account.

Do not send your password, verification codes, or other security credentials.

What happens after a request

BLOCKVERSE will process the account-deletion request and delete the BLOCKVERSE account and associated user data, subject to information that must lawfully be retained for security, fraud prevention or regulatory requirements.

Privacy

For information about how BLOCKVERSE handles personal information, see the BLOCKVERSE Privacy Policy.
