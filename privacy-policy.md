# BLOCKVERSE Privacy Policy

**Effective date: 22 September 2026**

BLOCKVERSE is a block puzzle and multiplayer game.

## Information we use
BLOCKVERSE may process Firebase account information such as email address and Firebase user identifier; player/profile information; game scores, XP, level, coins, achievements and daily progress; match/battle history; multiplayer room information, scores and moves; and chat messages and related metadata.

## Why we use it
We use this information to authenticate users, provide profiles, save gameplay and progression, provide matchmaking/Friend Match/real-time battles, provide chat and unread-message features, maintain match history, and provide support and account-management functions.

## Service provider
BLOCKVERSE uses Firebase services for authentication and cloud data storage. Data may be processed on infrastructure operated by the relevant service provider.

## Security
BLOCKVERSE uses authenticated Firebase access and Firestore security rules to restrict protected data. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

## Account deletion
Users can initiate account deletion from BLOCKVERSE Settings. Users who no longer have access to the app can request deletion through the external BLOCKVERSE account-deletion page.

**External deletion page:** Replace the placeholder URL on this page after hosting.

Deletion covers the BLOCKVERSE account and associated user data, subject to information that must lawfully be retained for security, fraud prevention or regulatory requirements.
